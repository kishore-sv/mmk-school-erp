# Performance landing

- Keep marketing pages as Server Components.
- Do not add carousel or animation packages for decoration.
- Static-render module, feature, integration, and solution routes.
- Lazy-load nothing above the fold that affects layout.
- Avoid duplicate client form logic; share one enquiry component.
