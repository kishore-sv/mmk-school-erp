# Tailwind landing

- Content width: `max-w-6xl` inside horizontal padding `px-4 sm:px-6`.
- Section rhythm: `py-16 md:py-24`. Heading to body: `mt-3` or `mt-4`. Card padding: `p-5` or `p-6`.
- Use semantic colours (`bg-background`, `text-muted-foreground`, `border-border`).
- Do not use `bg-gradient-*`, gradient text, blur blobs, or heavy shadows.
- Stack sections on small screens. Avoid horizontal overflow.
